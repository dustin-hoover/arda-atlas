# Mountain ranges and their Earth analogues

Each range's colours on the map come from a real range of like height, latitude and climate, sampled from NASA's
Blue Marble: Next Generation (July, public domain) by `tools/analogues/sample.py`. Only a few colours per range are kept
(forest, meadow, bare rock, and how much bare rock shows); no imagery is copied. Google Maps imagery is not used: its terms
forbid copying or deriving data from it.
